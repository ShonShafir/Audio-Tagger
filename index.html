Built for messy libraries.

Stop editing tags one by one. Audio Tagger gives you the power of a spreadsheet combined with automated metadata retrieval.

🧠 Smart Filename Parsing

Automatically extracts Artist, Featured Artist, Title, Remixers, and Catalog Numbers from the ugliest filenames.

💿 Discogs Integration

Fetch high-quality cover art, release dates, labels, genres, and styles directly from the official Discogs API.

📊 Spreadsheet Editor

Review, edit, and apply tags in bulk. Toggle column visibility instantly using modern pill buttons to keep your workspace clean.

🎯 Targeted Operations

Highlight specific cells to selectively re-parse filenames or fetch metadata without overwriting your manual edits elsewhere.

🔍 Bulk Find & Replace

Fix recurring typos or update artist names across hundreds of tracks at once with a live preview of the changes.

⚠️ Duplicate Detection

Instantly spots identical proposed filenames and highlights them in amber to prevent file overwrite collisions before they happen.

📜 M3U Playlist Integration

Automatically scans `.m3u` files in your directories to assign missing Catalog Numbers to unidentifiable tracks.

🎵 Built-in Audio Player

Double-click any track in the grid to preview the audio instantly using the built-in floating media player.

⚙️ Fully Customizable

Set up your own custom file naming template, configure static fallback values (like Country or Genre), and tailor the app to your workflow.
